Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What is the minimum deposit requirement?
The minimum deposit to activate your account is $500. For Premium accounts, we recommend a minimum balance of $5,000 to access all features.
How long does KYC verification take?
KYC verification typically takes 1-2 business days. Our automated system processes most applications within 24 hours.
What currencies are supported?
We support USD, EUR, GBP, and BTC. Premium members can access additional currencies including JPY, CHF, and CAD.
Are my funds insured?
Yes, client funds are held in segregated accounts with partner banks and are protected by FDIC/FSCS insurance up to applicable limits.
Can I access my account from anywhere?
Yes, our platform is accessible globally via web and mobile apps. You can manage your finances from anywhere in the world.
What happens if my balance exceeds $20,000?
Starter accounts have a $20,000 balance limit. If your balance exceeds this amount via deposits, transfers, or admin additions, your account will be frozen until you upgrade to Premium.
How do I upgrade to Premium?
Upgrade to Premium for a one-time fee of $1,000. You can upgrade via deposits, transfers, or by contacting support. Premium removes balance limits and unfreezes frozen accounts.
How do I unfreeze my account?
To unfreeze your account, upgrade to Premium ($1,000 one-time). Contact support for assistance with the upgrade process. Once upgraded, your account will be unfrozen and balance limits removed.
